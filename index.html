Theme Hospital

Bullfrog's 1997 classic, running in your browser on the open-source CorsixTH engine. You bring the game files; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Use different game files

  1. Own it? Theme Hospital is sold on GOG. After installing it, pick the game's folder below.
  2. Or download the MS-DOS copy from the Internet Archive (Theme_Hospital_1997.zip, about 225 MB) and pick the zip. A CD image (.iso) works too.
  3. Just trying it? The free official demo (13 MB) has one level. Pick its zip.

Your hospital is saved in this browser. In the game, the ☰ button at the top right (or Ctrl+F8) lets you download or import saves and change game files. Works best in a recent Chrome or Edge.

An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Electronic Arts or Bullfrog. Runs CorsixTH (MIT licence). This page hosts none of the original game's files. Source.
